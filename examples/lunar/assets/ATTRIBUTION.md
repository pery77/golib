# Original assets

All artwork, map geometry, shader code, sounds and music in SELENE: Frontier
were created for this game. No third-party art, fonts or audio were downloaded.

- Planet illustrations and icon: generated by the visible standard-library Go
  program in `sources/art/main.go`.
- Editable level geometry: Tiled object maps in `assets/maps/`.
- Ships, terrain facets, colonies and interface: drawn with GoLib in `art.go`.
- Ambient score and synthesized effects: `sounds.go`.
- Font: GoLib's built-in font, covered by its framework distribution.
