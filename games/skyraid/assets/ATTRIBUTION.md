# Where these files come from

Everything in this folder was made for Sky Raid, except what is listed here.
`golib dist` copies this file into the game's THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt, so
every file that came from somewhere else belongs here, with its license.

## music/drozerix_-_building_energy.xm

"Building Energy", a tracker module by Drozerix, made in MilkyTracker between
2015 and 2019, from The Mod Archive, module 185456 (the module itself carries
the author's contact):
<https://modarchive.org/index.php?request=view_by_moduleid&query=185456>

Its license is the one that module's page gives: write it here, in the page's
own words, before sharing the game.

## textures/background.png

Made with ChatGPT (OpenAI) for this game. What may be done with a picture
generated there is what OpenAI's terms say at the time it was made: check them
before sharing the game, and write here what they allow.

## sounds/*.jfxr

Made for this game in jfxr (<https://jfxr.frozenfractal.com>), from its own
presets. The files hold the settings, not the sound: GoLib makes the sound
from them.
