# Attribution

## models/

The FBX files in `models/` are from an FBX pack the game's author bought, made from the **Platformer Kit** by Kenney, <https://www.kenney.nl>, under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0): <http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>. Copied unchanged from the pack:

- `astronaut.fbx`: the character, with its skin and no animation; its texture, the kit's `colormap.png`, is inside the file.
- `idle.fbx`, `walk.fbx`, `sprint.fbx`, `jump.fbx`, `fall.fbx`, `punch.fbx`, `die.fbx`, `emote-yes.fbx`, `pick-up.fbx`: one animation each, on the character's armature, without the character.
- `coin-gold.fbx`, `tree.fbx`, `tree-pine.fbx`, `rocks.fbx`, `flag.fbx`, `block-grass-large.fbx`, `flowers.fbx`: props, each with the kit's texture inside.
